## 📌 Executive Summary
Project 3 represents the **data extraction and querying phase** of our analytics training pipeline. Moving beyond static spreadsheet manipulation, this project demonstrates pure relational logic and declarative querying to convert raw e-commerce transaction data into executive business intelligence.

Using **SQLiteStudio**, this project addresses strategic business questions regarding overall financial scale, inventory demand, high-value transaction auditing, checkout behavior, and marketing acquisition ROI.

## 🎯 Business Objectives & Problem Scenarios

Rather than writing arbitrary queries, every SQL script in this project was authored to solve a specific operational challenge:

1. **Macroeconomic Portfolio Health**
   * **Challenge:** Executive leadership cannot make strategic decisions by reviewing 1,200 individual raw transaction records.
   * **Objective:** Establish high-level baseline metrics for gross revenue, total transaction volume, Average Order Value (AOV), and unique customer reach.

2. **High-Value VIP Order Auditing**
   * **Challenge:** High-stakes purchases carry elevated financial and customer satisfaction risks.
   * **Objective:** Isolate premium transactions ($\ge \$2,000$) that have completed shipping for quality assurance and delivery tracking verification.

3. **Category Performance & Demand Planning**
   * **Challenge:** Merchandising teams need data-driven direction on product demand to prevent stockouts and reallocate warehouse space.
   * **Objective:** Evaluate order frequencies, total unit sales, gross revenue, and average revenue per transaction across all product categories.

4. **Tier-1 Flagship Performance Analysis**
   * **Challenge:** Row-level filtering cannot evaluate post-aggregation metrics, making it difficult to isolate top-tier product lines.
   * **Objective:** Filter grouped categorical output to display exclusively top-performing product lines generating over $\$180,000$ in revenue.

5. **Payment Gateway & Checkout Optimization**
   * **Challenge:** Finance and product teams require visibility into payment preferences to manage processing fees and optimize checkout flows.
   * **Objective:** Measure transaction distribution and average order sizes across Credit Card, Debit Card, and Online payment methods.

6. **Marketing Acquisition & Channel ROI**
   * **Challenge:** Marketing budget allocation requires clear attribution regarding which channels drive customer growth.
   * **Objective:** Analyze unique customer acquisition and total channel revenue generated by Instagram, Email, Facebook, and Referral channels.

## ⚙️ Technical Methodology & Database Architecture

* **Database Management System:** SQLite / SQLiteStudio
* **Source Dataset:** Gold-Standard Cleaned E-Commerce Dataset (`Cleaned_Dataset_Project1.csv`)
* **Target Table:** `Sales_1` (1,200 verified rows, 0 null cells, 0 duplicates)

### Key Architectural Concepts Applied:
* **Declarative Querying:** Defining the required data output while allowing the database engine's Cost-Based Optimizer (CBO) to determine execution paths.
* **Logical Execution Order:** Structuring queries according to internal processing mechanics (`FROM/JOIN` $\rightarrow$ `WHERE` $\rightarrow$ `GROUP BY` $\rightarrow$ `HAVING` $\rightarrow$ `SELECT` $\rightarrow$ `ORDER BY`) to avoid execution traps such as using column aliases within `WHERE` clauses.
* **Row vs. Aggregate Filtering:** Employing `WHERE` for row-level evaluation prior to grouping, and `HAVING` for filtering aggregated metric buckets.
